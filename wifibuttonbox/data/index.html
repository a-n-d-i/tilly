Reading the diagram

  • Two nested loops: an outer angle loop (heading error → desired turn rate, gain ATC_STR_ANG_P) and an inner rate loop (PID + feed-forward tracking that turn-rate demand against measured yaw rate).
  • The live numbers under the PID and output stages are an instantaneous snapshot at t≈0 (integrator empty, no slew history) — they show direction and rough scale, not a settled trajectory.
  • Tuning order that generally works well: 1) get ATC_STR_RAT_FF/P/D tracking a rate step cleanly, 2) add ATC_STR_RAT_I to kill steady-state error, 3) raise ATC_STR_ANG_P for a crisper outer loop, 4) use ACC_MAX/DEC_MAX and SMAX to tame overshoot without detuning the PID itself.

About this page

  • Served directly by the buttonbox's ESP32 — this page and its telemetry feed both come from the boat's WiFi, no separate backend to run.
  • GCS_PID_MASK (bit 0, Rover steering axis) is set automatically on boot so PID_TUNING data flows without manual setup.
  • The firmware re-reads the 17 tuning parameters every few seconds so the Signal Flow tab's steppers stay in sync with whatever's actually flashed, including changes made from another GCS.
